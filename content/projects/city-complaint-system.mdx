## Problem

Roorkee's municipal body needed a way for citizens to file complaints and
for each complaint to reach the department responsible for it. A complaint
that arrived without the details a department needed meant staff had to
go back to the citizen before anyone could act on it.

## Approach

The goal was the simplest system that closes the loop. A citizen files a
complaint online, the system routes it to the right department, and the
complaint moves through clear handling states until it is resolved. I
built it on WordPress, using custom PHP for the complaint workflow,
JavaScript for the interactive front end, and MySQL for the data.

I built the citizen facing reporting form with validation on both the
client and the server, so missing details were caught before a complaint
reached a department.

## Architecture & Tech

WordPress serves as the CMS backbone, with custom PHP for complaint
intake, routing, and status tracking, backed by MySQL. I designed the
relational schema for complaint records and their handling states, from
submission through routing to resolution. The front end is HTML, CSS, and
JavaScript.

## Results & Impact

- The system launched for Roorkee's municipal body and routed about 300
  citizen complaints a month to the responsible departments.
- Because complaints reached the department queues complete, they needed
  fewer follow ups with citizens for missing details.
- At handover, I trained the administrative staff on the response
  workflow.

This project taught me what production software involves: real users, real
data, and real consequences when something breaks.

## What I'd do next

Today I would build it as a Next.js and Node application with a PostgreSQL
backend, SMS notifications for status changes, and an analytics view that
shows the city where complaints cluster.
